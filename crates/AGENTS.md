# Rust Crates Development Guide

## turbo_tasks caching and invalidation

Read and follow the shared [turbo_tasks caching and invalidation guidance](../turbopack/AGENTS.md#turbo_tasks-caching-and-invalidation). Those turbo_tasks rules also apply throughout `crates/` and all its subdirectories when developing with turbo_tasks.

## Rust conventions

The [Rust conventions](../turbopack/AGENTS.md#rust-conventions) in `turbopack/AGENTS.md` also apply throughout `crates/`. For building, testing, and benchmarking, use `$rust-turbopack`.
