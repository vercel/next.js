Use Next.js DevTools to inspect errors, route information, and loading states while running `next dev`.

## Open DevTools

Click the Next.js logo (**N**) to open the DevTools menu. The logo appears in the bottom-left corner by default. The menu includes route information, [Preferences](#preferences), and tools such as the [Navigation Inspector](/docs/app/guides/instant-navigation#visualize-loading-states-with-the-nextjs-devtools), which lets you preview loading states with Cache Components enabled.

When Next.js reports a diagnostic, click the red **Issues** or amber **Insights** button beside the logo to open its tab. You can also select **Diagnostics** from the menu.

## Move the badge

If the badge covers part of your app, close any open DevTools panels and drag the Next.js logo to another corner. You can also choose a corner under [**Position**](#position) in Preferences or set the initial corner with [`devIndicators.position`](/docs/app/api-reference/config/next-config-js/devIndicators).

## Collapse the badge

Click the chevron (**‹**) at the end of the badge to collapse it to the Next.js logo. The diagnostic count stays visible. Click the logo to expand the badge, then click it again to open the menu.

If the idle indicator is hidden, **×** dismisses the diagnostic badge until a new diagnostic appears. Build errors stay visible until you fix them.

## Preferences

Open **Preferences** from the DevTools menu to change how DevTools look and behave.

### Theme

Use your system theme, or choose **Light** or **Dark**.

### Position

Choose any of the four corners. Dragging the Next.js logo updates the same setting.

### Size

Choose **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** for the badge and DevTools panels.

### Show indicator

Turn off **Show indicator** to hide the idle badge and dismiss the current diagnostic. New errors and Insights still appear, and build errors stay visible.

To bring the badge back, click **Undo** on the confirmation, use your [shortcut](#keyboard-shortcut), or restart the dev server. You can also reopen Preferences from a new diagnostic and turn **Show indicator** back on. To hide the idle badge for the whole project, set [`devIndicators: false`](/docs/app/api-reference/config/next-config-js/devIndicators) in `next.config`.

### Keyboard shortcut

Click **Record shortcut** and press a key combination. Once it shows **Saved**, press the same keys to show or hide the indicator.

### Restart dev server

Restart the development server from the browser.

### Reset bundler cache

Clear the bundler cache and restart the dev server. Use this if you see stale errors or your changes don't appear.
